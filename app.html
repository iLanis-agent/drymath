Why the sticker lies

Dehumidifier pints are rated at 18.3 C and 60% RH - a warm, humid lab. Basements are cold, and a compressor's capacity collapses with temperature: about three quarters at 14 C, half at 10 C, and below that the coils spend their life defrosting, which is what desiccant units exist for. Meanwhile mold does not wait for puddles: it needs only 60%+ sustained humidity. The honest sequence is measure the RH, size for the coldest the room gets, then buy the class above the number, never the one on sale.
